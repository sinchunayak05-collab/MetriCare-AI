# MetriCare AI

## AI-Powered Maternal Healthcare Companion

MetriCare AI is an AI-powered healthcare companion designed to support
rural pregnant women by providing pregnancy tracking, nutrition guidance,
health reminders, and early risk alerts.

## Problem

Rural pregnant women may face challenges such as limited healthcare
access, missed checkups, lack of health awareness, poor nutrition,
and difficulty identifying pregnancy-related warning signs.

## Solution

MetriCare AI provides:

- Pregnancy tracking
- Health and medicine reminders
- Nutrition guidance
- AI-based health assistance
- Risk alerts
- Family/healthcare-worker notification
- Offline support

## Target Users

- Rural pregnant women
- ASHA workers
- Family members
- Primary healthcare workers

## Technologies

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Mobile Application
- Cloud Database



## Future Scope

- Integration with healthcare facilities
- Multilingual voice assistance
- Improved risk prediction
- Integration with wearable devices
